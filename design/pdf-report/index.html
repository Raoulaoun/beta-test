Red flags

No red flags found

Some patterns tell an investor more than any score. We check every statement for them. Martingale, grid trading or a blown account caps your score at 40, whatever the profit.

MartingaleClearDoubling size after each loss to win it back. One bad run wipes out the account. We found no losing streak followed by stepped-up size.
Grid tradingClearStacking orders at fixed intervals with no stop. Looks steady until the market trends. We found no clusters of evenly spaced open positions.
Blown accountClearA drawdown that took the account close to zero, or a margin stop-out. Your deepest drawdown was 18%.
Revenge sizingWatchNot a cap, but worth fixing. After a loss, you trade 1.6× bigger. Investors read this as emotion driving size. It is fix 1 in your plan.
Why these cap your score These strategies can show a smooth record for months and then lose everything in days. An investor can't see the risk in the result, so the score doesn't reward the result while they are present.

Your plan

Your top 3 fixes, step by step

1
Keep your size flat after a loss · DISCIPLINE
  1. Set one position size per account and write it down before the week starts.
  2. After any losing trade, the next trade uses the same size or smaller.
  3. After two losses in a day, stop trading until the next session.
2
Put a stop on every trade · DISCIPLINE
  1. Decide where you are wrong before you enter, and place the stop with the order.
  2. Never widen a stop once the trade is open.
  3. Review any trade without a stop at the end of each week.
3
Bring risk per trade to 1% · RISK CONTROL
  1. Size each position from the stop distance, not from a fixed lot.
  2. Use the free position size calculator before every entry.
  3. Cap total open risk at 3% across all positions.
